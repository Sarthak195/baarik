MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT — DESIGN CONSULTANCY

This Master Services Agreement is made on 20 February 2026

BETWEEN

Bluepeak Consumer Brands Private Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies
Act, 2013, having its registered office at Unit 1104, Parinaam Business Park, Saki
Vihar Road, Powai, Mumbai – 400072, Maharashtra (the "Client"),

AND

Mr. Arjun Nair Krishnan, sole proprietor trading as "Studio Ochre", having his place
of business at 27/1130, Mamangalam, Kochi – 682025, Kerala (the "Consultant").

1. SERVICES

1.1 The Consultant shall provide brand identity, packaging and digital design services
to the Client as described in each statement of work issued by the Client and accepted
by the Consultant (each, an "SOW").

1.2 The Consultant shall perform the Services in accordance with the Client's brand
guidelines, feedback and instructions, and shall be available for review calls on
reasonable notice.

2. REVISIONS

2.1 The Consultant shall carry out such revisions to each deliverable as the Client
may request, without limit as to the number of rounds, until the Client confirms that
the deliverable meets its requirements.

2.2 No revision requested under Clause 2.1 shall attract any additional fee, and no
revision shall extend the fee payable under the relevant SOW.

3. FEES

3.1 The fee for each SOW shall be as stated in that SOW. The fee for SOW-01 (brand
identity refresh) is Rupees Two Lakh Forty Thousand (Rs. 2,40,000) inclusive of all
expenses. The fee for SOW-02 (packaging system) is ₹1,80,000.

3.2 The Consultant shall raise an invoice only after the Client has confirmed, in
writing, that all deliverables under the SOW have been accepted.

4. PAYMENT

4.1 Acceptance of a deliverable and approval of an invoice shall be at the sole
discretion of the Client. The Client shall be under no obligation to state reasons for
withholding acceptance or approval.

4.2 The Client shall pay each approved invoice within ninety (90) days of the date on
which the Client approves that invoice.

4.3 The Client may withhold from any payment any amount which the Client in good faith
considers to represent a defect, delay or deficiency in the Services.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 All right, title and interest in every deliverable, work in progress, sketch,
concept, artwork, font pairing, source file and design element created by the
Consultant in connection with this Agreement, whether accepted by the Client or not,
shall vest absolutely in the Client with effect from the date of this Agreement.

5.2 The assignment at Clause 5.1 takes effect on the signature of this Agreement and
is not conditional upon payment of any fee.

5.3 The Consultant waives all moral rights in the deliverables and shall not display,
publish or reproduce any deliverable, or any part of it, in any portfolio, case study,
award entry or social media post, without the Client's prior written consent.

6. CONSULTANT'S OBLIGATIONS

6.1 The Consultant shall not, during the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12)
months thereafter, provide services of a similar description to any business engaged
in the personal care category in India.

6.2 The Consultant shall keep confidential all information of the Client and shall not
disclose it to any person.

7. INDEMNITY

7.1 The Consultant shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Client, its
directors, employees and affiliates against all claims, damages, losses, penalties,
costs and expenses of every description, including legal fees, arising out of or in
connection with the Services, the deliverables, any allegation of infringement, or any
act or omission of the Consultant.

7.2 The indemnity at Clause 7.1 is unlimited in amount and unlimited in time, and
shall survive the termination of this Agreement.

7.3 Nothing in this Clause 7 shall be read as subject to the limitation of liability
at Clause 12.4.

8. TERMINATION

8.1 The Client may terminate this Agreement, or any SOW, at any time and for any
reason, by written notice to the Consultant with immediate effect.

8.2 On termination under Clause 8.1, the Client shall have no liability to pay for any
deliverable, work in progress or service for which an invoice has not already been
approved under Clause 4.1, and the rights assigned under Clause 5.1 shall not be
affected by the termination.

8.3 The Consultant may terminate this Agreement only on ninety (90) days' written
notice and only after completing every SOW then in progress.

9. STATUS OF THE CONSULTANT

9.1 The Consultant is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates any
relationship of employment, partnership or agency, and the Consultant is not entitled
to any leave, provident fund, gratuity or other employment benefit.

9.2 The Consultant shall be responsible for his own taxes and statutory registrations.

10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

10.1 Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration by a
sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat of
arbitration shall be Mumbai.

10.2 The courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction, subject to Clause 10.1.

11. NOTICES

11.1 Notices shall be given in writing by e-mail to the addresses stated in the
relevant SOW, and shall be deemed received on the day of transmission.

12. GENERAL

12.1 This Agreement, together with each SOW, constitutes the entire agreement between
the parties.

12.2 The Client may amend the Client's design and delivery policies from time to time,
and the Consultant shall comply with the policies then in force.

12.3 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.

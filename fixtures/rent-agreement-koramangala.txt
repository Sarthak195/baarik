LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT

This Leave and Licence Agreement is made at Bengaluru on the 12th day of January 2026

BETWEEN

Mr. Harish Devendra Pai, aged about 58 years, son of Late Shri Devendra Pai,
permanently residing at "Shanta Nivas", Kalsanka Road, Udupi – 576101, Karnataka,
hereinafter called the "LICENSOR",

AND

Ms. Sneha Bhattacharya, aged about 27 years, daughter of Shri Amitava Bhattacharya,
presently employed at Bengaluru and residing at Bengaluru, hereinafter called the
"LICENSEE".

WHEREAS the Licensor is the absolute owner of Flat No. 402, 4th Floor, "Neelkanth
Elegance", 8th Main Road, 5th Block, Koramangala, Bengaluru – 560095, admeasuring
about 1,150 square feet of super built-up area, together with one covered car parking
slot (the "Licensed Premises"), and has agreed to grant a licence to occupy the same
on the terms set out below.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

1. GRANT AND PERIOD

1.1 The Licensor grants to the Licensee a licence to occupy the Licensed Premises for
a period of eleven (11) months commencing on 1 February 2026 and ending on 31 December
2026.

1.2 This Agreement creates a licence only and does not create any tenancy, lease,
sub-lease or any interest in the Licensed Premises in favour of the Licensee.

1.3 Renewal, if any, shall be at the sole discretion of the Licensor by a fresh
agreement, and the Licensee shall have no right of renewal.

2. LICENCE FEE

2.1 The Licensee shall pay a monthly licence fee of Rupees Thirty Thousand
(Rs. 30,000) in advance, on or before the 5th day of every English calendar month, by
electronic transfer to the Licensor's designated bank account.

2.2 On every renewal of this Agreement, or on the expiry of eleven (11) months,
whichever is earlier, the monthly licence fee shall stand increased by ten per cent
(10%) over the licence fee then payable, without any further agreement between the
parties.

2.3 If the licence fee for any month is not received by the 5th day of that month,
interest at the rate of three per cent (3%) per month shall become payable on the
outstanding amount until it is paid in full.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT

3.1 The Licensee has, on or before the execution of this Agreement, paid to the
Licensor an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rupees Three Lakh
(Rs. 3,00,000), being the equivalent of ten (10) months' licence fee, the receipt of
which the Licensor acknowledges.

3.2 The Licensor shall be entitled to adjust from the security deposit any arrears of
licence fee, any electricity or water charges, any amount claimed under Clause 6, and
any sum which the Licensor considers necessary to restore the Licensed Premises.

4. LOCK-IN PERIOD

4.1 The parties agree that the entire period of eleven (11) months set out in
Clause 1.1 shall be a lock-in period.

4.2 If the Licensee vacates the Licensed Premises, or ceases to pay the licence fee,
at any time before the expiry of the lock-in period, the entire security deposit of
Rupees Three Lakh (Rs. 3,00,000) shall stand forfeited to the Licensor absolutely, in
addition to any other amount then due, and the Licensee shall have no claim to any
part of it.

5. USE OF THE LICENSED PREMISES

5.1 The Licensed Premises shall be used for residential purposes only, by the Licensee
and her immediate family, and by no other person.

5.2 The Licensee shall not sub-licence, assign, part with possession of, or permit any
paying guest in the Licensed Premises.

5.3 The Licensee shall not keep any pet, carry on any commercial activity, or use the
Licensed Premises for any social or religious gathering.

5.4 The Licensee shall not make any alteration, fixture or addition, including the
installation of an air-conditioner, water purifier or mounting bracket, without the
prior written consent of the Licensor.

6. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE

6.1 The Licensee shall, at her own cost, keep the Licensed Premises in good order and
shall attend to and pay for all repairs of every description, whether internal or
external, ordinary or structural, and whether arising from the Licensee's use or from
the age or condition of the building, including plumbing, sanitary fittings, electrical
wiring, waterproofing, painting and repairs to the walls, roof and slab.

6.2 On the expiry or earlier determination of this Agreement, the Licensee shall hand
over the Licensed Premises freshly painted and in the same condition in which it was
handed over to her, normal wear and tear not excepted.

7. UTILITIES AND OUTGOINGS

7.1 The Licensee shall pay the electricity and water charges of the Licensed Premises
as per the meter readings, and the monthly association charges levied by Neelkanth
Elegance Owners' Association, directly to the concerned authority.

7.2 Property tax shall be borne by the Licensor.

8. RIGHT OF ENTRY

8.1 The Licensor, or any person authorised by him, shall be entitled to enter the
Licensed Premises at any time, without notice to the Licensee, to inspect the Licensed
Premises, to carry out any work, or to show the Licensed Premises to prospective
licensees or purchasers.

9. DETERMINATION BY THE LICENSOR

9.1 Notwithstanding anything contained in Clause 4, the Licensor may determine this
Agreement at any time by giving the Licensee fifteen (15) days' written notice, if in
the opinion of the Licensor the Licensee has committed a breach of any term of this
Agreement.

9.2 On such determination the Licensee shall vacate the Licensed Premises within the
said fifteen (15) days, failing which the Licensee shall pay damages for use and
occupation at three (3) times the daily licence fee for every day of continued
occupation.

10. STAMP DUTY AND REGISTRATION

10.1 The cost of stamp duty and of registration of this Agreement, if any, shall be
borne wholly by the Licensee.

11. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

11.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.

11.2 The courts at Udupi alone shall have jurisdiction in respect of any dispute
arising out of this Agreement.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have signed this Agreement on the day, month and year
first written above.
